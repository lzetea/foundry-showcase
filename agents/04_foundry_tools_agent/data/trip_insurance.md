# Contoso Travel — Trip Insurance

## Plans

### Essential (USD 49 per trip)
- Trip cancellation up to USD 2,500
- Trip interruption up to USD 2,500
- Medical emergency up to USD 50,000
- Baggage loss up to USD 1,000

### Plus (USD 89 per trip)
- Trip cancellation up to USD 10,000
- Trip interruption up to USD 10,000
- Medical emergency up to USD 250,000
- Baggage loss up to USD 2,500
- Trip delay coverage (USD 200 per day, max 5 days)

### Premier (USD 159 per trip)
- Trip cancellation up to USD 25,000
- Trip interruption up to USD 25,000
- Medical emergency up to USD 1,000,000
- Baggage loss up to USD 5,000
- Trip delay coverage (USD 500 per day, max 10 days)
- Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR) — 75% reimbursement
- Rental car collision damage waiver

## Covered reasons for cancellation

- Illness or injury of traveller, travel companion, or immediate family member
- Death of traveller, companion, or immediate family
- Natural disaster at origin or destination
- Military deployment orders
- Terrorist incident at destination within 30 days of travel
- Jury duty or subpoena
- Bankruptcy of a supplier (airline, hotel, tour operator)

## Exclusions

- Pre-existing medical conditions (unless waived with purchase within 14 days
  of initial trip deposit)
- Change of mind (covered only under CFAR in the Premier plan)
- Pregnancy-related claims after 26 weeks of gestation
- Travel to regions under a Level 4 travel advisory at time of booking
- Injuries sustained during extreme sports (unless Premier Adventure rider is
  purchased)

## Filing a claim

Claims must be submitted within **90 days** of the incident via the Contoso
Travel mobile app or the customer portal. Required documentation includes
receipts, medical reports (for health claims), and police reports (for theft
claims).
