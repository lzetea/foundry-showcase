# Contoso Travel — Visa Requirements Quick Reference (2026)

> This is a summary for common Contoso Travel destinations. Always confirm
> requirements with the destination consulate before travel.

## Visa-free or visa-on-arrival

The following destinations allow **US, EU, UK, Canadian, Australian, and
Japanese passport holders** to enter without a pre-arranged visa for short
stays (typically 30–90 days):

- Japan (90 days visa-free)
- South Korea (90 days visa-free)
- Singapore (90 days visa-free)
- Thailand (30 days visa-on-arrival)
- United Arab Emirates (30 days visa-on-arrival)
- Mexico (180 days visa-free)
- Schengen Area (90 days within any 180-day period — ETIAS authorization
  required from 2025 onwards for visa-exempt nationals)
- United Kingdom (6 months visa-free — ETA required from 2025)

## Electronic authorization required

- **USA**: ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) — USD 21, valid
  2 years, required for Visa Waiver Program countries.
- **Canada**: eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization) — CAD 7, valid 5 years.
- **Australia**: ETA / eVisitor — AUD 20, valid 12 months.
- **New Zealand**: NZeTA — NZD 17, valid 2 years, plus IVL fee NZD 100.
- **EU Schengen**: ETIAS — EUR 7, valid 3 years.
- **UK**: ETA — GBP 10, valid 2 years.

## Visa required (pre-arranged)

Examples of destinations requiring a visa in advance for most Western
passport holders:

- China (tourist L-visa — USD 140, processing 4 business days)
- India (e-Tourist Visa — USD 25 for 30 days, USD 40 for 1 year)
- Russia (tourist visa — USD 160, invitation letter required)
- Saudi Arabia (tourist e-visa — USD 110, valid 1 year)
- Vietnam (e-visa — USD 25, 90 days single-entry)

## Passport validity

Most destinations require **at least 6 months of remaining passport validity**
from the date of entry. Check specific country rules before booking.

## Transit visas

Some countries require transit visas even for airside connections:

- **USA**: no true transit; even airside transit requires ESTA or a C-1 visa.
- **UK**: Direct Airside Transit Visa required for certain nationalities.
- **China**: 72/144-hour visa-free transit available at major cities.
- **Schengen Area**: Airport Transit Visa required for certain nationalities.

## Special advisories

- Travelers with passport stamps from countries on certain sanctions lists
  may be denied entry to others (e.g. Israel → some Arab League states).
- Dual nationals should consult both consulates; some destinations require
  entry/exit on the same passport.
