# Contoso Travel — Baggage Policy (2026)

## Carry-on allowance

| Cabin class | Carry-on bags | Personal item | Max weight per bag |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|--------------------|
| Economy     | 1             | 1             | 7 kg / 15 lb       |
| Business    | 2             | 1             | 10 kg / 22 lb      |
| First       | 2             | 1             | 12 kg / 26 lb      |

Carry-on dimensions must not exceed **55 × 40 × 23 cm** (22 × 16 × 9 in).

## Checked baggage allowance

| Cabin class | Included bags | Max weight per bag | Max linear dimensions |
|-------------|---------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Economy     | 1             | 23 kg / 50 lb      | 158 cm / 62 in        |
| Business    | 2             | 32 kg / 70 lb      | 158 cm / 62 in        |
| First       | 3             | 32 kg / 70 lb      | 158 cm / 62 in        |

## Excess baggage fees

- Overweight (23–32 kg): **USD 100** per bag, per segment.
- Overweight (32–45 kg): **USD 200** per bag, per segment.
- Oversized (>158 cm linear): **USD 150** per bag, per segment.
- Each extra bag beyond the included allowance: **USD 125** per bag, per segment.

## Restricted items

The following items are **not permitted** in checked or carry-on baggage:
compressed gases, lithium batteries above 100 Wh, flammable liquids/solids,
corrosive materials, oxidizers, infectious substances.

## Special items

- **Sports equipment** (skis, golf clubs, bicycles): included in the standard
  allowance if within 23 kg and 203 cm linear. Excess charged at USD 150.
- **Musical instruments** ≤ 75 cm: free as a carry-on (replaces the standard
  carry-on). Larger instruments require a purchased seat.
- **Mobility aids** (wheelchairs, scooters): carried free in addition to the
  standard allowance.
