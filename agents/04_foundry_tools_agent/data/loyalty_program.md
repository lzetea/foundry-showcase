# Contoso Travel — Loyalty Program ("Contoso Elite")

## Tier structure

| Tier       | Qualifying miles (per year) | Qualifying segments |
|------------|-----------------------------|---------------------|
| Silver     | 25,000                      | 30                  |
| Gold       | 50,000                      | 60                  |
| Platinum   | 75,000                      | 90                  |
| Diamond    | 125,000                     | 140                 |

## Earning miles

- **Base earning rate** (Economy): 5 miles per USD spent on airfare.
- **Business class**: 8 miles per USD.
- **First class**: 11 miles per USD.
- **Hotel partners**: 3 miles per USD on prepaid bookings via Contoso Travel.
- **Car rental partners**: 2 miles per USD on qualifying rentals.

## Tier benefits

### Silver
- Priority check-in
- 1 free checked bag (Economy)
- 25% bonus miles on all earnings

### Gold
- Silver benefits
- Lounge access (2 guest passes per year)
- Complimentary upgrades when available
- 50% bonus miles
- Free Wi-Fi on flights

### Platinum
- Gold benefits
- Unlimited lounge access with 1 guest
- Guaranteed upgrade on domestic flights (Business class, availability
  confirmed 72 hours before departure)
- 75% bonus miles
- Waived change/cancellation fees

### Diamond
- Platinum benefits
- Global First lounge access
- Guaranteed upgrade on any flight when paid at full fare
- 100% bonus miles
- Dedicated 24/7 concierge line
- Four systemwide upgrades per year

## Redemption

- **Award flights** start at 7,500 miles one-way for short-haul Economy.
- **Transatlantic Business** redemptions: from 62,500 miles one-way.
- **Hotel nights**: from 20,000 miles per night at partner properties.
- Miles expire after **24 months** of account inactivity.
